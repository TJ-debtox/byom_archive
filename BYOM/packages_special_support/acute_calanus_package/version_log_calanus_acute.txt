Version log Calanus acute package for BYOM

===========================================================================
What is it?

This package contains the files needed to reproduce the results of the paper "Dynamic links between lipid storage, toxicokinetics and mortality in a marine copepod exposed to dimethylnaphthalene" (Jager et al., 2017). Standard GUTS analyses, TK analysis with a one- and two-comp. model, and combined GUTS and two-comp. TK analysis.

- Jager T, Øverjordet IB, Nepstad R and Hansen BH (2017). Dynamic links between lipid storage, toxicokinetics and mortality in a marine copepod exposed to dimethylnaphthalene. Environ Sci Technol. 51(13):7707-7713. http://dx.doi.org/10.1021/acs.est.7b02212 (accepted version and SI can be found with the entry on https://www.debtox.info/papers_survival.html).
